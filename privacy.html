User agreement and privacy policy

Version 1, 2026-11-22. Crypten for Android shows it before its first use, and again whenever it changes; accepting it is how you start.

Free and non-commercial

Crypten is free, for everyone, and made without any commercial purpose: no price, no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases. Any support you show us is voluntary and unlocks nothing; Crypten works the same without it.

Offline: nothing leaves this device

Crypten works without the internet and has no permission to use it, so it cannot send anything anywhere — and it does not: no account, no servers, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reports. Its makers never see your keys, your passwords, your files or how you use it. Nothing leaves Crypten unless you export it, or open a file with another app, yourself.

What Crypten keeps, and where

Your key files, workspaces, settings and activity history stay in Crypten’s private storage on this device, left out of Android’s backups. If you choose a backup folder, an encrypted copy of your key files and workspaces is kept there too, brought up to date after every save; an export you make goes where you choose. Neither ever holds a password or anything decrypted. A password is never stored, unless you turn on unlocking with your fingerprint or face: then it is kept encrypted under a key of Android’s Keystore that only your fingerprint or face opens, on this device only. Uninstalling Crypten, or clearing its storage, deletes everything it keeps — but not your backup folder.

The permissions it uses

Notifications (optional), to show the progress of a running task; keeping a task you started running while you use another app; and your fingerprint or face, only if you turn that on. Crypten never asks for the internet, your storage, contacts, location, camera or microphone: you hand it each file or folder yourself, in Android’s file picker.

Your responsibility

You use Crypten at your own knowledge and risk, and you alone are responsible for how you use it: for any misuse, for losing a key file or forgetting a password, for losing files, and for any damage that follows — including data corrupted by a fault in Crypten. Crypten is tested on every kind of file and in every situation its makers could think of, precisely to keep that risk low, but no software is free of faults.

Lost means lost

Without the key file and its password, nothing Crypten encrypted can be decrypted — not by you, not by anyone, not by Crypten’s makers. Keep copies of every key file in safe places, keep your passwords safe, and keep backups of the files that matter to you.

No warranty

Crypten is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind. As far as the law allows, its makers are not liable for any loss or damage that comes from using it, or from being unable to use it.

The law where you are

You are responsible for using Crypten lawfully, including any rules on encryption in your country.

Changes and questions

If this agreement changes, Crypten shows it to you again before you go on. Questions: write to obi.tirips@gmail.com. No one from Crypten will ever ask you for a password, a key file or your data.
